# Business Requirements - Enterprise AI Transformation Case Study

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for a fictional AI-transformation assessment at **NovaTech Industries GmbH**.

The goal is not to design a production AI system. The goal is to show how I would structure the early business-analysis work before a company starts investing in multiple AI pilots.

## 2. Business Objective

NovaTech wants to identify AI use cases that can create measurable business value without starting with projects that are too complex, too risky, or poorly supported by data.

The assessment should help management answer four questions:

1. Where can AI create useful business value?
2. Which ideas are realistic enough to pilot first?
3. What risks and stakeholders must be considered?
4. How could the portfolio be phased over roughly 12 months?

## 3. Scope

The assessment covers eight functions:

- Finance
- HR
- Procurement
- Customer Service
- Supply Chain
- Sales
- Operations
- IT

A total of 24 synthetic AI use cases are assessed.

## 4. Functional Requirements

### BR-01 - Use Case Register
The project must maintain one consistent register of AI opportunities with a clear use-case ID, business problem, proposed AI approach, business owner and implementation assumptions.

### BR-02 - Comparable Scoring
Every use case must be scored on the same dimensions so that opportunities can be compared consistently.

### BR-03 - Value and Feasibility Balance
The model must not rank ideas only by potential value. Feasibility, data readiness, effort, risk and ROI should influence prioritization.

### BR-04 - Quick-Win Identification
The output must identify ideas that combine high priority with relatively high feasibility, manageable implementation effort and manageable risk.

### BR-05 - Roadmap
Selected opportunities should be grouped into practical phases rather than treated as 24 projects that start at the same time.

### BR-06 - Risk Visibility
The project must document common AI risks and practical mitigations.

### BR-07 - Stakeholder Ownership
Each department should have a clear executive sponsor, process owner, data owner and IT partner.

### BR-08 - Pilot Success Measures
Roadmap items should include a measurable pilot outcome such as time saved, routing accuracy, forecast improvement or request deflection.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

- The scoring logic should be understandable to a non-technical business stakeholder.
- All numbers in the case study must be synthetic.
- GenAI use cases should retain human review where wrong outputs could affect people, customers, finance or legal decisions.
- Sensitive information should only be used through approved, access-controlled systems.
- The model should be easy to challenge and adjust in a workshop.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

The case study is considered complete when:

- 24 use cases are documented.
- Every use case has a priority score.
- High-priority and quick-win opportunities are visible.
- A phased implementation roadmap is available.
- Stakeholders and key AI risks are documented.
- At least one business process is shown in current-state and future-state form.

## 7. Assumptions

This is an educational portfolio project. NovaTech Industries GmbH is fictional, and none of the value, cost, risk or timing estimates represent a real organization.
